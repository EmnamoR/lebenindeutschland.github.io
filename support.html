About EinburgerungTest

EinburgerungTest is designed to help you prepare for the German citizenship test (Einbürgerungstest). The app contains practice questions covering German history, politics, society, and law to help you succeed on the official exam.

How to Use the App

  • Start Practice: Choose from different question categories or take a full practice test
  • Review Answers: See detailed explanations for each question
  • Track Progress: Your scores and progress are automatically saved
  • Bookmark Questions: Save difficult questions to review later
  • Study Offline: All content works without internet connection

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this the official test?
A: No, this is a practice app to help you prepare. You must take the official test at an authorized testing center.
Q: Are the questions authentic?
A: The questions are based on the official test format and cover the same topics you'll encounter on the real exam.
Q: How many questions are in the app?
A: The app contains hundreds of practice questions covering all test categories.
Q: Is my progress saved?
A: Yes, all your progress is automatically saved on your device. No account required.
Q: Does the app work offline?
A: Yes, once downloaded, the app works completely offline. No internet connection needed.
Q: Can I use this app in other German states?
A: Yes, the citizenship test is standardized across all German states (Bundesländer).

Troubleshooting

App won't start or crashes:

  • Force close the app and restart it
  • Restart your device
  • Make sure you have the latest app version
  • Free up device storage space

Questions not loading:

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Check if you have enough storage space
  • Restart your device

Progress lost:

  • Progress is stored locally on your device
  • Uninstalling the app will delete all progress
  • Progress cannot be transferred between devices

Reset all data:

  • Uninstall and reinstall the app to start fresh
  • Or look for a "Reset Progress" option in app settings

System Requirements

  • 50 MB available storage space
  • Compatible with iPhone and iPad
