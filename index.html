Privacy Policy for EinburgerungTest

Last updated: September 24, 2025

Quick Summary

We do not collect any personal information. This app works completely offline and all your study progress is stored only on your device.

About This App

EinburgerungTest is a German citizenship test preparation app that helps users study for the official Einbürgerungstest. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name or personal identification
  • Email addresses or contact information
  • Test scores or study progress (this stays on your device)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • IP addresses

Local Data Storage

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your quiz results and progress
  • App settings and preferences
  • Bookmarked questions

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Security

Since we don't collect any data, there's no personal information at risk. All your study data is protected by your device's built-in security features.

Children's Privacy

This app is safe for users of all ages. We do not collect any information from children or adults.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how the app works to collect data, we will update this policy and notify users through an app update.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect your data, there's nothing for us to delete or modify. You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app from your device.
